# Open Questions

1. Is the EVM implementation intended to reach production independently, or to
   remain an executable reference for the Kaspa implementation?
2. Which real collateral assets and decimal configurations are in scope?
3. Which oracle providers, quorum, heartbeat, deviation, and fallback policy are
   acceptable?
4. Who are the intended multisig signers and what threshold is required?
5. Should accumulated fees strengthen reserves, fund the treasury, or be split?
6. What legal entity, jurisdiction, reserve attestation, and redemption
   obligations apply before any public stablecoin launch?
7. What is the required testnet duration and value-at-risk ceiling?
